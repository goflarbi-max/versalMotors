# VersalMotors - Business Questions

Management wants to understand performance across sales, inventory, service and finance.

1. Which car models and trims generate the highest revenue but have the lowest profit margin?

2. Which branches/dealerships have declining sales month-over-month and why?

3. Which vehicle models have rising warranty claim costs over the last 12 months?

4. What is our average inventory aging - which cars have been unsold the longest in each branch?

5. Which salespersons/teams consistently achieve above-target sales and what are they selling?

6. Which service centers have the highest customer complaints or repeat repairs?

7. What is the trend of after-sales revenue vs vehicle sales revenue by branch?

8. Which customer segments (individual vs corporate) bring the most profitable deals?

9. Which models have the highest discount rate to sell, and is it affecting margin?

10. What is the monthly cash flow forecast based on current inventory and pending sales?
